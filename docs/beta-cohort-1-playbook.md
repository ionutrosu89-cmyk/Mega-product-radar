# Mega Product Radar — External Beta Cohort 1 Playbook

## Objective
Validate the commercial product with 5–10 real Romanian e-commerce sellers before a wider public launch. Do not fabricate participants, feedback, willingness-to-pay, conversions, checkout outcomes, or product results.

## Who we recruit
Aim for a mixed cohort:
- 1–2 beginners who are considering e-commerce
- 2–4 active eMAG / Trendyol / own-store sellers
- 1–2 sellers importing or planning to import from China
- 1–2 experienced operators who can challenge the product critically

Avoid recruiting only friends who are likely to be polite. We need useful disagreement.

## Invitation message
Salut! Lucrez la Mega Product Radar, o aplicație pentru sellerii din România care îi ajută să treacă de la semnale de produs la o decizie comercială mai disciplinată: Discover → Radar → dosar comercial → TEST/HOLD → plan de lansare.

Suntem într-o etapă beta și caut 5–10 selleri care să o testeze ca utilizatori reali. Testul durează aproximativ 15–25 minute. Nu vreau feedback „de complezență”; mă interesează unde este neclar, ce nu ai folosi și dacă ai plăti pentru anumite funcții.

În Launch există și acces la un agent de sourcing din China testat/verificat practic; serviciile efective ale agentului se contractează separat.

Dacă vrei să participi, îți trimit linkul și traseul scurt de test.

## Participant instructions
Do not explain every screen before the test. We want to learn what the product communicates by itself.

Ask the participant to:
1. Open the public landing page.
2. Create an account / log in.
3. Complete Onboarding V2 and Plan Finder.
4. Note which plan is recommended and whether the explanation feels credible.
5. Open Home.
6. Explore Discover and open at least one product in Radar.
7. Open the Commercial Dossier.
8. Explain in their own words why the product is HOLD / TEST / BUY.
9. Add at least one product to Watchlist.
10. Open Pricing / Launch and review the China-agent benefit.
11. Submit beta feedback.

## Moderator rules
- Do not tell the participant what the correct answer is before they answer.
- Do not interpret HOLD for them until after you ask what they think it means.
- Do not claim that estimated or derived data are verified sales.
- Do not encourage payment just to create a conversion.
- Do not run paid scans solely to make the demo look better.
- Record bugs separately from feature ideas.

## Questions to ask after the journey
### Comprehension
- In one sentence, what do you think Mega Product Radar does?
- Which screen gave you the most value?
- Where did you hesitate or get confused?
- What do VERIFIED, ESTIMATED and DERIVED mean to you?
- Why was the product you opened HOLD / TEST / BUY?

### Commercial value
- Would this save you time in a real product-sourcing decision? How?
- Would this reduce the chance of investing in a weak product? Why or why not?
- Which plan would you choose today: Free, Discover, Radar or Launch?
- What would you expect to pay monthly for the plan you would choose?
- What would stop you from paying today?

### Launch / China agent
- Does access to a tested/verified China sourcing contact materially increase the value of Launch?
- Did you understand that access to the contact is included but the agent's sourcing / negotiation / inspection / logistics work is contracted separately?
- Would this benefit make you choose Launch over Radar? Why?

## Metrics to capture
- onboarding completed: yes/no
- recommended plan: FREE / DISCOVER / RADAR / LAUNCH
- participant agrees with recommendation: yes/no + reason
- Discover opened: yes/no
- Radar opened: yes/no
- Commercial Dossier opened: yes/no
- Watchlist add: yes/no
- understands HOLD: yes/no
- understands VERIFIED / ESTIMATED / DERIVED: yes/no
- Pricing opened: yes/no
- stated plan preference
- stated willingness to pay (only explicit participant statement)
- China-agent benefit: low / medium / high value
- critical bug encountered: yes/no + description

## Cohort 1 success criteria
Product-learning targets, not marketing claims:
- no critical auth, navigation or data-loss failures
- majority complete onboarding without assistance
- majority understand that HOLD means insufficient proof, not automatically a bad product
- several participants organically reach a Commercial Dossier and Watchlist
- enough explicit feedback on pricing and the China-agent benefit to decide whether positioning needs another iteration

## GO / NO-GO review after the cohort
Choose one evidence-based outcome:
- GO: run Cohort 2 with minimal changes
- ITERATE UX: comprehension or navigation issues dominate
- ITERATE PRICING: value is understood but plan/value perception is weak
- ITERATE DATA: trust in evidence quality is the main blocker
- NO-GO PUBLIC LAUNCH: critical reliability or billing issues remain

Public launch readiness remains separate from beta readiness. Preserve all nine commercial money gates and the existing TEST/HOLD/BUY discipline.
