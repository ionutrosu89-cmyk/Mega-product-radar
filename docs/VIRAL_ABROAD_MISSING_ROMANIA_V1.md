# Viral Abroad / Missing in Romania V1

This subsystem detects generic or small-brand product concepts that accelerate in foreign markets before Romania becomes crowded.

## Funnel

`foreign observation -> generic concept -> repeated history -> cross-platform confirmation -> brand gate -> Romania validation -> importability -> 3 suppliers -> economics -> finalist review`

Viral evidence is discovery evidence, never proof of sales, Romanian scarcity, importability, supplier reliability, margin or purchase authorization.

## Initial markets

- Early discovery: US, GB
- European confirmation: DE, FR, IT, ES, PL
- Target validation: RO

## Source activation

All source records start disabled and `REVIEW_REQUIRED`. A collector may be enabled only after its access method, terms, rate limits, provenance fields and retention policy are approved. Paid providers remain disabled without explicit approval.

## Scoring

- TikTok velocity: 25%
- Meta advertising momentum: 15%
- Google acceleration: 20%
- Amazon demand: 15%
- foreign-country spread: 10%
- validated Romania scarcity: 15%

Missing Romania validation contributes zero rather than an optimistic scarcity score. At least two observations and two platforms are required. Established brands stop before scoring.

## Operational milestones

1. Activate compliant Google Trends and YouTube official collectors.
2. Run a 14-day US/GB pilot with daily snapshots.
3. Normalize branded creatives into generic product concepts.
4. Send only `ACCELERATING`/`VIRAL` concepts confirmed in two countries to Romania validation.
5. Permit commercial validation only with direct, fresh Romania evidence.
